Who We Are

Hi, I'm Quintus 👋

Welcome to Quintie Pintie's Printies — a cozy corner of the internet where 3D printing meets imagination. I'm Quintus, a passionate designer who creates original 3D files and brings them to life layer by layer.

This space is where I share photos of my printed creations, from intricate desk ornaments to functional everyday objects. Whether you're a seasoned maker or just 3D-curious, you'll find something here to inspire you.

Got a project in mind but not sure where to start? I love helping people figure out what's possible with 3D printing. Drop me a message — let's make something together!
